Chapter 1 · Visual language

Paper carries the work. Glass is chrome. Ceremony is a reward.

Five laws with reject power. If a screen breaks them, the screen is wrong — never the law. Everything in Chapters 2–5 is this chapter, tokenized.

The five laws

01 · Ground

Paper carries the work. Cards, lists, KPIs live on paper with a hairline — never glass, never gems.

Reject — a Spline gem or glass blur on an opportunity card.

02 · Hue

Sapphire acts. Amethyst measures. Red-beryl scores. Citrine warns. Nothing else is a hue.

Reject — a decorative teal chip count instead of var(--role-action).

03 · Type

Type is weight and size. Muted steps down the neutral ramp — never a decorative hue for hierarchy.

Reject — coloring a timestamp amethyst to make it “secondary”.

04 · Depth

Hairline elevates in-flow UI. Shadow is for depth viz and controls floating on imagery only.

Reject — giving a KPI pill var(--shadow-sm) instead of a hairline.

05 · Motion

Motion explains state. Ceremony may boast. The feed may not.

Reject — a looping gem shimmer on the opportunities feed.
