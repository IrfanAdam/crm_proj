Chapter 1 · Visual language

High output, by design.

Businesses grow when their people hit goals. So the system turns tracking into momentum — gamified goals, a nudge toward the next step, and a reward when the work lands. Everything on this page is that idea, made visible.

What it values

  1. 01

    Tangible and transparent. Every number traces back to real work — 4/32 closed, 23km away, last contact 2d. Nothing decorative, nothing vague.

    Reject — a dashboard that shouts.

  2. 02

    Fresh and friendly. Minimal, spaced, quiet surfaces — the tool feels like a colleague, not a ledger.

    Reject — density that reads as surveillance.

  3. 03

    Celebration is earned. Rock Solid Goals, Golden Streak, Focus Master, Momentum — gems appear only where a win happened, never as decoration.

    Reject — a gem on an opportunity card.

What it's made of

Ceramic — the quiet work surface

Cards, lists, KPIs. Matte, calm, built for long days.

Aluminium — the precise frame

Dock, rails, machined edges. The new-age device holding it all.

Gems — the reward

Sapphire, citrine, amethyst, red beryl. Kept in their box until someone wins.

How a day moves through it

Alert → Progress → Study → Planning → Sales → Review

  1. AlertThe day opens itself — nudges, not a blank screen.
  2. ProgressWhere the numbers stand, one glance.
  3. StudyDrill into the funnel, see what moved.
  4. PlanningThe next steps suggest themselves.
  5. SalesThe work happens out in the world.
  6. ReviewCome back, see what the work did, celebrate.

How it speaks

Celebration · boasts
