Chapter 1 · Visual language

Ceramic holds the work. Aluminium frames it. Gems celebrate it.

Three materials, five laws, three screen recipes. Every law below can reject a screen — if a screen breaks one, the screen is wrong.

The five laws

  1. 01

    Ground — ceramic holds the work. Cards, lists, KPIs are glazed ceramic: matte, hairline seams, never a shadow.

    Reject — a glass blur or a gem on an opportunity card.

  2. 02

    Hue — the signal light decides. Sapphire is the device's LED: primary action, selected tab, chip count. Amethyst measures, red-beryl scores, citrine warns distance.

    Reject — a decorative teal for a count.

  3. 03

    Type — every screen opens with the same display title. Big, 32, bold — then card title 20, body 16, meta 14, micro 12. Size 24 is reserved, never a title.

    Reject — a 24px title, or an amethyst timestamp faking hierarchy.

  4. 04

    Depth — seams, not shadows. In-flow UI elevates with a 1px hairline. Shadow is reserved for depth viz and controls floating on imagery.

    Reject — a shadow on the KPI pill.

  5. 05

    Motion — motion explains a state change. The feed states; only celebration boasts.

    Reject — a looping shimmer on the feed.

Materials — what each surface is made of

Ceramic — content surfaces

Cards, lists, KPIs. Matte glazed white, quiet. Witness ref-01, 02, 03, 09.

Aluminium — the device frame

Dock, rails, machined edges. Brushed, precise, new-age. Witness prototype dock; supersedes the ref-01 flat tab.

Gems — celebration, kept in its box

Rock Solid Goals (sapphire, ref-08) · Golden Streak (citrine) · Focus Master (amethyst) · Momentum (red beryl). Never in the feed.

◆
Peak marker — a diamond blink, not a gemstone

Sits on peaked bars. A measure accent, never a gem.

Screen recipes — the build order

Three screens, one grammar. Each step below names the piece, then says what it is.

Operate · the everyday screen — your pipeline at a glance

  1. Display title + avatarThe screen's name in big type, your face at right for presence.
  2. KPI strip — 4/32 closedThe scoreboard: where the number stands, one glance.
  3. Hero visualOne big picture of the territory: map or funnel.
  4. Delta lineWhat moved since yesterday: +32.
  5. Chips, hot gets 2px inkFilters. The active filter carries a 2px ink underline.
  6. Ceramic cards, hairlineThe deals themselves — matte cards, hairline seams, no shadow.
  7. Aluminium dock, 3 tabsThe device frame at the bottom: Home, Leads, Opportunities.

Monitor · the funnel readout — where every deal stands

  1. Display title + avatarSame opening, same title, every screen.
  2. Funnel hero, 7 statesThe 3D funnel, from first touch to retained — tap a stage, the card expands.
  3. Metrics — 32%, 4.9Conversion and NPS, side by side.
  4. Timeline chips + NowY/Q/M/W/D slices plus Now — switching reshapes the funnel.

Reports · the review — patterns over time

  1. Display titleSame opening, no avatar — this screen is about the numbers.
  2. Matrix tabs M/Q/YTime slices: month, quarter, year.
  3. Blob-vs-Goals gridActivity dots against goal bars — effort vs target.
  4. Business chipsThe lens: Business or Performance.

A missing step needs a PR note. Home and Leads omit only via the one empty state — never by dropping the title.
