Components · Cards · 01 OpportunityCard

One row's full story. Never a floating panel.

OpportunityCard is the Opps row: who, stage, chance, value, next touch. It tells the deal's state at a glance; it never hosts a second primary action — icon actions only.

  • 01

    Anatomy — logo · who · stage rail · chance + value · segs · contacts · foot. 44px round __logo, __stage-name on a sapphire-400 rail with a 3px left rule, __chance red-beryl beside __value sapphire, __segs segment bar, __stack contacts, __foot meta + citrine __dist.

    Reject: a second primary button — foot carries 44px icon __actions only.

  • 02

    Hues are data, never decoration. Sapphire = stage, red-beryl = chance, citrine = distance datum. Content drives emphasis; there is a single variant, no decorative recoloring.

    Reject: swapping chance to sapphire or distance off citrine-700 — the mapping is the contract.

  • 03

    Hairline frame, zero shadow. border.thin frame on surface; cards never float and never lift on hover — only action buttons take the wash.

    Reject: any box-shadow on the card — shadow law holds for every card.

Do

Keep sapphire = stage, red-beryl = chance, citrine = distance. Keep the card an article with a heading. Keep actions 44px icons.

Don't

Never shadow the card. Never add a second primary button. Never recolor hues decoratively.
