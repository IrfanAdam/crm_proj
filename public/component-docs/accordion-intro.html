Components · Navigation · 04 Accordion

Stacked detail on demand. Never hidden chrome.

Accordion expands sections in place — record detail blocks, grouped filters, help text. It reveals and collapses; it never navigates. Use single-expand for exclusive sections, multi-expand for independent ones.

  • 01

    Anatomy — trigger · icon · panel. .accordion__trigger carries aria-expanded + aria-controls; .accordion__panel is hidden when shut. Chevron .accordion__icon trails, leads under .accordion--chevron-leading.

    Reject: a panel with no labelled trigger — every region answers to its header.

  • 02

    Single or multi, same CSS. Single-expand closes siblings in JS; multi-expand leaves items independent. Mode is behaviour, never a second stylesheet.

    Reject: two accordion skins for two modes — behaviour varies, chrome does not.

  • 03

    Motion earns its keep. Panels animate grid-template-rows 0fr ↔ 1fr over --motion-duration-md; chevrons rotate 180°. Under prefers-reduced-motion it snaps instantly.

    Reject: pixel-height keyframes — snap, don't slide.
