Components · Navigation · 03 Pagination

Position in a list. Never a wall of numbers.

Pagination moves through long lists — deals, activities, results. It pages and announces place; it never hides how much remains. Use full paging for tables; use compact prev/next where the slot is narrow.

  • 01

    Anatomy — nav · pages · status. nav aria-label="Pagination" holds prev, numbered .pagination__btn, next, plus .pagination__ellipsis and .pagination__status. Current carries aria-current="page".

    Reject: numbers without a status line — every control answers “where am I”.

  • 02

    Ellipsis compresses, compact strips. Distant ranges collapse to one …; .pagination--compact drops numbers for a joined prev/next strip. Status stays — compression never deletes orientation.

    Reject: a compact strip with no status — fewer buttons means the words matter more.

  • 03

    Disabled, never vanished. Unavailable nav keeps its slot with disabled + --opacity-disabled so layout holds. Active page is action-ink, not a link away.

    Reject: hiding prev on page one — ends disable, they never disappear.
