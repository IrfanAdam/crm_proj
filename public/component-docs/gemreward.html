Components · Ceremony · 05 GemReward

One gem, four moods. Never in Operate.

GemReward is the achievement ceremony: a single Spline iframe recolored per category through a hue-rotate API, standing on a capsule with Rock Solid Goals copy. Celebration only — quarantined from Operate and Monitor.

  • 01

    Anatomy — spline · title · capsule · copy. One __spline iframe (my.spline.design/gem-embed), __title, __capsule stand (+Rs 4lakh · Beyond Target), __copy slots. One iframe, never four embeds.

    Reject: a second Spline embed per category — recolor, don't re-embed.

  • 02

    Ceremony-only quarantine. Gems appear in achievement moments; mood never enters Operate/Monitor chrome, KPI fills, or measure visuals.

    Reject: a gem beside a KPI or inside Operate — ceremony leaking into chrome.

  • 03

    Hue-rotate API, reduced-motion exit. Category maps to filter: sapphire 0°, citrine 38° + 1.1, red-beryl 310° + 1.2, amethyst 265° + 1.2. Reduced-motion drops to no filter.

    Reject: animating or recoloring the gem under reduced-motion — the fallback is instant and unfiltered.

Do

Use one Spline iframe with the hue-rotate map. Title the iframe. Provide the per-category SVG fallback. Keep mood quarantined.

Don't

Never place gems in Operate/Monitor. Never use gem hues as chrome. Never skip the reduced-motion fallback. Never embed four iframes.

Accessibility — the non-negotiables

Iframe carries a title per category · fallback SVG when Spline fails · reduced-motion means no hue-rotate and no entrance · copy stays real text.
