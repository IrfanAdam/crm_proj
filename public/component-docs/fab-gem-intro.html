Patterns · Dock · 01 Floating gem

A blob with a memory of home.

The floating gem is the dock's action button — one tap target that squishes under the press, stretches where it's dragged, and wobbles back to its anchor on release. It is the dock's one moving part; it never detaches.

  • 01

    Feel is state, not choreography. Press, lag and return are integrated from the same spring pair every frame — the blob's shape at any instant is just where the gesture is. Constants ride the element as --fab-elasticity · --fab-damping.

    Reject: keyframed bounces that play the same whether you flick or crawl — motion that ignores input reads dead.

  • 02

    Stretch follows lag. Elongation only ever points along the vector the blob trails, and the cross-axis squashes to half — the goo is a consequence of being late, never a decoration. Full stretch at 95px of lag, capped by --fab-stretch.

    Reject: uniform scale pops — that's a zoom, not a drag.

  • 03

    It always wobbles home. Release snaps the blob to its dock anchor on an underdamped spring (--fab-damping, ζ 0.50 default → one clear overshoot). A tap keeps its action; a drag never repositions it.

    Reject: dropping the blob where it lands, or a leader line back to a lost anchor.
