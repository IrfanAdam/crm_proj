Patterns · Dock · 01 Floating gem

A blob with a memory of home.

The floating gem is the dock's action button — one tap target that squishes under the press, strains toward the finger without ever leaving its dock, and wobbles back to its anchor on release. It is the dock's one moving part; it never detaches.

  • 01

    Feel is state, not choreography. Press, lag and return are integrated from the same spring pair every frame — the blob's shape at any instant is just where the gesture is. Constants ride the element as --gooey-elasticity · --gooey-damping · --gooey-give.

    Reject: keyframed bounces that play the same whether you flick or crawl — motion that ignores input reads dead.

  • 02

    Stretch follows pull. Elongation only ever points from the blob toward the finger (smoothed), and the cross-axis squashes to half — the goo is strain, never decoration. Full stretch at 95px of pull, capped by --gooey-stretch.

    Reject: uniform scale pops — that's a zoom, not a drag.

  • 03

    It holds its dock, then wobbles home. Travel is resisted — at most --gooey-give px toward the finger, so it never follows the pointer across the screen — and release snaps back on the underdamped spring (--gooey-damping, ζ 0.50 default → one clear overshoot). A tap keeps its action; a drag never repositions it.

    Reject: letting the blob travel with the finger, or dropping it where it lands.
