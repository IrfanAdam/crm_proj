Patterns · Deck · 01 Data Utility

Three plates, one geometry, one word.

The deck is the DS's hero object — three squircle glass plates floating in a white void, a wordmark resting on the top face. It makes "data, stacked and translucent" a thing you can look at: hierarchy from opacity, depth from the air gaps, and a camera that arcs out and rests.

  • 01

    One geometry, three opacities. Every plate is the same slab (deck-form.js — radius 0.27w, bevel ≈ ¼ of thickness); hierarchy is the opacity ladder alone — top clear → base saturated, all off the sapphire ramp (--primitive-sapphire-ui-200/300/400).

    Reject: three hand-tuned meshes that drift apart on the next edit.

  • 02

    The azimuth locks at 45°. The diamond read is the pattern — motion is elevation + dolly only: contact → read → settle (the reference arcs out and rests). Stations are data (DECK_SCENE.STATIONS), never keyframed poses.

    Reject: turntable orbits that never settle on the word.

  • 03

    The void stays void. No ground shadow, no stroke lines: the rim is clearcoat + the studio env, and bloom is depth-gated to geometry so the white stage never glows.

    Reject: glow sprites floating in whitespace and painted edge outlines.
