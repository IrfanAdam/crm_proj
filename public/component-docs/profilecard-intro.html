Components · Cards · 03 ProfileCard

One face, one role, three proofs.

ProfileCard is the person card: centered avatar, name and role, three stat proofs over a hairline rule. It introduces a person; detail lives on their page.

  • 01

    Anatomy — avatar · name · role · stat trio. 64px round __avatar, __name + __role, __stats of three __stat cells (__val + __label) divided by a hairline top rule.

    Reject: a fourth stat — the trio is the contract, not a starting point.

  • 02

    Centered, always. Avatar, name, role and stats share one central axis; no left-aligned variant exists — alignment is identity, not preference.

    Reject: a left-aligned ProfileCard — the center gate holds.

  • 03

    Hairline frame, zero shadow. border.thin on bg.surface with radius.lg; avatar ring and stat rule reuse the same hairline weight; card never floats.

    Reject: any box-shadow or a heavier stat rule — one hairline throughout.
