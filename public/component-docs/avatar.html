Components · Identity · 05 Avatar

Person marks. Round only, always named.

Avatar is the person mark — initials fallback or photo, with presence and stacking. Round always, never square or squircle. Initials when there is no photo; cover-fit photo when there is.

  • 01

    Anatomy — round frame · initials or img · optional presence. Five sizes from avatar--xs (20px) to avatar--xl (64px); md 32px is the base. Initials are 1–2 semibold chars; photos use object-fit: cover and never crop the face.

    Reject: a square or squircle avatar — round is the law.

  • 02

    Stacks overlap by one step with a surface ring. .avatar-stack piles faces with a one-step negative overlap; every stacked face and every presence dot carries the surface ring so marks never bleed together.

    Reject: a stack or dot without the surface ring.

  • 03

    Presence is decorative — the name does the talking. avatar--online (green dot) and avatar--offline (dim dot) never mean availability alone in text contexts; status is announced in text or an accessible name.

    Reject: presence color as the only availability signal.

Snippet — the contract in code

<span class="avatar avatar--md avatar--online" aria-label="Ada Lovelace — online">AL</span> <span class="avatar-stack"><span class="avatar avatar--sm">AL</span><span class="avatar avatar--sm"><img src="grace.jpg" alt=""></span></span>
Do

Use initials when there is no photo. Keep the surface ring on stacks and dots. Overlap stacks by one spacing step. Give clickable avatars the person's name.

Don't

Never crop faces. Never square the frame. Never dot without the ring. Never signal availability by dot color alone.

Accessibility — the non-negotiables

Decorative photos carry alt=""; initials are text, not image · presence dots are decorative — announce status in text or aria-label (e.g. aria-label="Online") · clickable avatars are native <button> / <a> named with the person name · visible focus ring.
