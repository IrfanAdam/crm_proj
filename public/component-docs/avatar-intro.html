Components · Identity · 05 Avatar

Person marks. Round only, always named.

Avatar is the person mark — initials fallback or cover-fit photo, with presence and stacking. Round always, never square or squircle. Use it when a person needs a face in a row; reach for Icon when the mark is action, StatusPill when the mark is stage.

  • 01

    Anatomy — round frame · initials or img · optional presence. Five sizes avatar--xs (20px) to avatar--xl (64px) via spacing.5 / 6 / 8 / 12 / 16; base md 32px; initials 1–2 semibold; photo object-fit:cover; frame radius.full + bg.interactive.

    Reject: a square or squircle avatar — round is the law.

  • 02

    Stacks overlap by one step with a surface ring. avatar-stack piles faces with negative spacing.2 overlap; every stacked face and every presence dot carries a bg.surface ring via border-width.medium.

    Reject: a stack or dot without the surface ring.

  • 03

    Presence is decorative — the name does the talking. avatar--online green-300 dot and avatar--offline dim dot never mean availability alone; status is announced in text or the avatar accessible name.

    Reject: presence color as the only availability signal.
