Components · Navigation · 05 TabBar

Three icons home. Never a fourth wheel.

TabBar is the mobile dock — icon-only Phosphor glyphs with 44px+ targets that jump between top-level homes. It docks and signals; it never labels. At three or fewer tabs it supersedes Tabs — never render both.

  • 01

    Icon-only dock, fat targets. Glyphs only, no labels; each .tabbar__item clears 44px (--spacing-10 min) inside a --bg-surface-glass bar. Active is .tabbar__item--active mirrored by aria-current="page".

    Reject: labelled dock items or sub-44px targets — thumb-first, text-free.

  • 02

    One signal dot, ever. Single .tabbar__signal dot in --role-action on active only. Counts live in separate .tabbar__badge pill (--role-score), omitted at zero — badge and signal never twin.

    Reject: badge plus signal on one item — one item, one mark.

  • 03

    Three tabs kill the tab row. At ≤3 tabs on mobile the dock supersedes Tabs for same views. Two switchers for one set is disorientation with extra steps.

    Reject: dock and tab row side by side — pick the dock, drop the row.
