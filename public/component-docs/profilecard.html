Components · Cards · 03 ProfileCard

Face, role, three proofs. Never a résumé.

ProfileCard is the person card — the Olivia pattern: 64px avatar, name plus role, a trio of proof stats over a hairline rule. It introduces a person; detail lives on their page.

  • 01

    Anatomy — avatar · name · role · stat trio. 64px round __avatar, __name + __role, __stats of three __stat cells (__val + __label) divided by a hairline top rule.

    Reject: a fourth stat — the trio is the contract, not a starting point.

  • 02

    Centered, always. Content centers in the frame; avatar, name, role and stats share one axis. No left-aligned variant exists.

    Reject: a left-aligned ProfileCard — alignment is identity, not preference.

  • 03

    Hairline frame, zero shadow. border.thin on surface; avatar ring and stat rule reuse the same hairline. The card is static.

    Reject: any box-shadow or a heavier stat rule — one hairline weight throughout.

Snippet — the contract in code

Do

Keep exactly three stats. Keep everything centered. Leave the photo alt empty — the name is text.

Don't

Never left-align the card. Never add a fourth stat. Never shadow the frame.

Accessibility — the non-negotiables

Photo carries alt="" (name is adjacent text) · stats are plain text pairs, never color-only · static card takes no focus · centered order stays logical.
