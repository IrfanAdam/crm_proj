Chapter 2 · Foundations · 05 Shadow

Hairlines carry the UI. Shadow is for depth.

Cards float on white by hairline, not by shadow — restraint is the brand. Shadow is spent only where depth means something: the funnel you can tilt, the gem you just earned. If everything casts a shadow, nothing is elevated.

  • 01

    Cards use hairlines, never shadow — --border-thin on white separates every card.

    Reject: a drop shadow under an opportunity card — decoration posing as structure.

  • 02

    Shadow marks visualization depth — funnel tilt, gem capsules, overlays.

    Reject: shadow on a static list row — there is no depth to show.

  • 03

    Three steps only — sm for lift, md for float, lg for ceremony; mark for dark-on-dark.

    Reject: a custom blur radius — depth is a scale, not a slider.

  • 04

    Dark theme deepens, never inverts — black-based shadows at higher alpha.

    Reject: reusing a light shadow on dark surfaces — it vanishes.
