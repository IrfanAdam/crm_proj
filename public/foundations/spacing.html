Chapter 2 · Foundations · 03 Spacing

Everything sits on a 4px grid.

Spacing is the quiet law: every pad, gap and section is a multiple of 4. Cards breathe at 16, sections break at 24 and 32. When two elements feel "slightly off", it is always the grid — never taste.

  • 01

    Multiples of 4, no exceptions — 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64.

    Reject: a 10px gap, a 20px pad — if it is not on the grid, it is a mistake.

  • 02

    Card padding is 16 — spacing.4 inside every card, every time.

    Reject: a 12px card that "felt tighter" — tightness comes from the grid, not around it.

  • 03

    Sections break at 24 / 32 — rhythm the eye can predict.

    Reject: three different section gaps on one screen.

  • 04

    Tokens, not numbers, in code — spacing.* everywhere.

    Reject: padding:16px — one rescale and every bare number lies.
