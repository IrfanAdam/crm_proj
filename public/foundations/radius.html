Chapter 2 · Foundations · 04 Radius

Corners tell you what a thing does.

A pill is a promise: this can be tapped, filtered, slid. A rounded rect is a container: it holds, it stays. Gem capsules are the only organic shape in the system — everything else is geometry. Read a corner and you know the behavior before you touch it.

  • 01

    Full-pill means transient — chips, bars, filters, toggles. If it can be tapped on and off, it is a pill.

    Reject: a pill-shaped card, a square chip.

  • 02

    Cards live at 16→24 — radius.lg default, radius.2xl for hero cards.

    Reject: a 4px card (reads as a button) or a fully round panel.

  • 03

    Small steps for small chrome — inputs and thumbs at radius.xs/sm/md.

    Reject: a 16px text input — the chrome outweighs the content.

  • 04

    Gem capsules stay organic — only rewards may break geometry.

    Reject: an organic blob behind a KPI — ceremony leaking into chrome.
