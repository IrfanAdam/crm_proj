Chapter 2 · Foundations · 02 Typography

Type carries hierarchy. Never color.

One family — Inter — five reading steps. Hierarchy comes from size, weight and line height, never from hue: a title is bigger and bolder, never bluer. Color-coded hierarchy dies in sunlight, in grayscale, and for every color-blind user.

  • 01

    Five steps, fixed jobs — title 32 · card 20 · body 16 · meta 14 · micro 12.

    Reject: a 24 or an 18 — steps exist so sizes never drift.

  • 02

    Weight pairs with the step — title and card bold, body regular, meta and micro medium.

    Reject: bold body copy, or a meta line shouting in semibold.

  • 03

    Line height belongs to the step — titles hold at 1.15, prose reads at 1.50, meta at 1.40.

    Reject: 1.15 on a paragraph — tight prose turns reading into line-hunting.

  • 04

    Quieter means --text-secondary — a lower ink step, never a lighter hue and never opacity.

    Reject: gray-blue captions, faded-sapphire timestamps.

  • 05

    Roles, not sizes, in code — components reference --type-*, never --font-size-* directly.

    Reject: a card title pointing at --font-size-xl — rename the scale and it orphans.
