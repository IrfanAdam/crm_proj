Chapter 2 · Foundations · 02 Typography

Type carries hierarchy. Never color.

One family — Inter — five steps. A title is bigger and bolder, never bluer. When hierarchy rides on color, it dies in sunlight, in grayscale, and for every color-blind user. Size and weight are the only levers.

  • 01

    Five steps, fixed jobs — title 32, card 20, body 16, meta 14, micro 12.

    Reject: a 24 or an 18 — steps exist so sizes never drift.

  • 02

    Weight pairs with step — title and card bold, body regular, meta and micro medium.

    Reject: bold body copy, or a meta line shouting in semibold.

  • 03

    Muted steps to secondary only — quieter means --text-secondary, never a lighter hue.

    Reject: gray-blue captions, faded-sapphire timestamps.

  • 04

    Roles, not sizes, in code — components reference --type-*, never --font-size-* directly.

    Reject: a card title pointing at --font-size-xl — rename the scale and it orphans.
