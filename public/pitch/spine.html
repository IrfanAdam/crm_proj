Narrative Framework
The AlphaGems story spine
01

The World Before

Sales was human. Every close was two people matching — interaction, fitness, identity all present.

02

The Rupture

Tools stripped that out. Interaction became logging, fitness became quota, identity became a number — and the human went missing.

03

The Insight

Three things were forgotten — interaction, fitness, identity. Restore those, and behaviour changes without enforcement.

04

The Solution

AlphaGems restores them quietly, in the background. The org defines what matters; every right behaviour is recognised in real time.

05

The World After

Better human behaviour upstream — honest interaction, genuine fit, owned identity. Cleaner pipeline downstream — data you can trust, forecasts that hold.
