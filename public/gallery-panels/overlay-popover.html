Spec — popover anchors

Rich anchored panel — keeps its trigger, holds links and buttons, non-modal. One focusable group, never chains.

Min 4 × --spacing-16, max 6 × --spacing-16 · --radius-md · --shadow-md · --z-index-dropdown · re-centers on scroll.

Spec — anatomy

.popover owns the surface, radius, and shadow; .popover__title (semibold, sm) names the panel, .popover__body (regular, sm, --text-secondary) holds detail. Absolute positioned from the anchor, like a menu.

Spec — states + a11y

Opens fade + scale over --transition-fast; Esc and outside clicks dismiss and return focus; focus may move inside and returns on close. Never paints a full scrim.

Spec — contract

Use for close-date pickers and rich detail that needs to stay anchored. Never popover chains or critical content.

Snippet + related <button aria-haspopup="dialog" aria-expanded="false">Close date</button> <div class="popover overlay--open" role="dialog" data-placement="bottom" aria-labelledby="t1"> <p class="popover__title" id="t1">Close date</p> <p class="popover__body">Anchored detail — links and buttons welcome.</p> </div>
