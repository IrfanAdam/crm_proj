Modal blocks for confirms only — one primary action, Tab trapped, Esc or scrim closes, focus returns to the opener. Reject: nested modals, auto-open on load, or a trap that strands focus.

Spec · sizes + states

Sizes --sm/md/lg/full, placement --top sheet / center / --bottom; open = scrim fade + content rise over --transition-fast, close = .is-closing to opacity 0, then hidden. Persistent --persistent ignores scrim + Esc. A11y: labelledby, focus in, Tab wrap, inert background.

Drawer keeps context for filters and long forms — it slides in, Esc or ✕ slides it back out, and the page behind stays visible. Reject: a modal where a drawer would keep context, or a drawer wider than --wide.

Spec · placements + motion

Sides --right/--left/--top/--bottom, widths --narrow/--wide; open = .drawer--open slide over --transition-spring, close rides the same curve back, reduced-motion snaps. Dismissible drawers trap like modals; persistent ones don't.

Menu — action list from a trigger

Anchored action list — opens under its trigger, arrows rove the items, a select or an outside click dismisses and refocuses the trigger. Reject: submenus past one level, icon-only items, or a menu standing in for a dialog.

Spec · anchors + a11y

Fade + scale from the anchor over --transition-fast, flips to fit, --in-dialog re-layers to modal z. A11y: role=menu/menuitem, roving arrows with Home/End, Esc closes and refocuses, no trap.

Tooltip — one name, pointer only

Hover or focus the button — the tooltip names one thing after a beat, fades out on leave, and never traps focus. Reject: a tooltip as the only accessible name, or one holding buttons.

Spec · placement + a11y

Placement [data-placement=top/bottom/left/right], auto-flip at the viewport edge, --wrap for touch long-press. A11y: role=tooltip + aria-describedby, Esc dismisses, never focusable, never blocks pointer.

Toast — transient confirm, always topmost

Toast confirms a past act — rises in, dwells 4s (8s with an action), pauses on hover, sinks out. Never takes focus. Reject: critical errors only in a toast, or a toast that steals focus.
