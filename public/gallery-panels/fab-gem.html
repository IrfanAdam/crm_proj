Spec — feel contract

Four live CSS variables on the element; attachGooey reads them on every press, so the lab retunes the real physics.

Variables — defaults, ranges, effect
VariableDefault · lab rangeEffect
--gooey-elasticity800 · 300–1800Spring stiffness (1/s²) — sets the give’s response and the snap-home tempo (ω = √k)
--gooey-damping0.50 · 0.2–1.0Release damping ratio ζ — 0.5 = one clear overshoot; 1.0 = dead stop (release only, drag stays 0.9)
--gooey-stretch0.26 · 0.05–0.45Max strain elongation, reached at 95px of pull; cross-axis squashes half of it
--gooey-squish0.08 · 0.02–0.20Press compression: +55% of it wide, full it flat; released on an underdamped pop (ζ 0.38)

--gooey-give · 14 · 0–80 — max travel toward the finger in px; quadratic onset keeps micro-tremor out, 0 = deformation only, fully anchored.

Spec — contract

One blob, one drive: position lag → stretch axis → press squish, composed in a single per-frame transform.
