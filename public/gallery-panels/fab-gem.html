Spec — feel contract

Four live CSS variables on the element; attachGooey reads them on every press, so the lab retunes the real physics.

Variables — defaults, ranges, effect
VariableDefault · lab rangeEffect
--fab-elasticity800 · 300–1800Follow stiffness (1/s²) — lag while dragging ≈ v·1.8/ω; ω = √k also sets snap-home tempo
--fab-damping0.50 · 0.2–1.0Release damping ratio ζ — 0.5 = one clear overshoot; 1.0 = dead stop (release only, drag stays 0.9)
--fab-stretch0.26 · 0.05–0.45Max elongation, reached at 95px of lag; cross-axis squashes half of it
--fab-squish0.08 · 0.02–0.20Press compression: +55% of it wide, full it flat; released on an underdamped pop (ζ 0.38)
