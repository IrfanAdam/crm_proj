Spec — anatomy + density

20px pill (min-width 20px) with bold micro numeral; --sm is 16px for icon anchors; --dot is fixed 8px circle with no text. Text stays single-line; tabular-nums freeze width under animation.

Spec — states + a11y

Static only — badge inherits anchor state; no hover, active, or focus of its own. Standalone badge is inline beside its label; overlap is absolute pinned with its own border as ring. Forced-colors flattens to Canvas / CanvasText.
