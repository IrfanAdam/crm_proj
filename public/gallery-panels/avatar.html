Five sizes — xs 20 to xl 64 — cover dense rows to profile headers. md 32 is the base; pick the smallest that keeps initials legible. Photo fills via cover, never stretches.

Spec — anatomy + density

Round frame (radius.full, 1px border.thin), centered semibold initials (1–2 chars) or img with object-fit:cover. Stack overlaps by one spacing.2 step; every stacked face carries a bg.surface ring via border.medium.

Spec — states + a11y

Presence dot is decorative — 8px top-right patch with bg.surface border; online green-300, offline dim. Clickable avatars hover border-accent; focus rings 2px sapphire.
