Ideal Customer Profile
Who buys AlphaGems — and who uses it

VP of Sales / CRO

Primary Buyer · Revenue Owner

Owns the number. Lives in pipeline reviews. Frustrated that CRM data is unreliable and rep attrition is killing forecast accuracy. Needs a lever that isn't a PIP or a comp restructure. Wants performance improvement without a culture overhaul.

"I need my team performing — and I need to trust the data they log."

RevOps / Sales Ops Lead

Technical Champion · Integration Owner

Manages the CRM, the automations, the reporting stack. Has spent months enforcing data hygiene through governance. Knows enforcement doesn't work at scale. Needs a pull mechanism, not another push policy. Evaluates the API integration and configuration depth.

"The data is dirty because no one is incentivized to keep it clean."

HR / People Lead

Influencer · Retention Advocate

Watching attrition climb in the sales org. Running generic engagement surveys that produce reports no one reads. Looking for something that ties recognition directly to performance in real time — not tenure, not vibes, not a once-a-year award.

"Our best reps are leaving because they don't feel seen."

Sales Professional

End User · Experience Owner

Logs into the CRM every day with low enthusiasm. Invests energy into deals and gets no real-time signal that it's working. Recognition arrives quarterly — if at all. Wants to feel the win at the moment it happens, not in a slide at the next all-hands.

"I closed a deal. Nothing happened."

End User · Experience Owner

Has minimal direct interaction with AlphaGems — most of it runs in the background. What they do see matters: a clear picture of which activities are earning rewards and how their performance stacks up. Focused on doing the right work and building the kind of track record that drives their own growth.

"I want to know I'm building something — not just hitting numbers."
