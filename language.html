Visual Language — paper · glass · ceremony

← Gallery · Prototype · spec visual-language.md

Principles

  1. Paper carries the work. Glass is chrome. Ceremony is a reward. Reject a gem or glass blur on an opportunity card.
  2. Sapphire acts. Amethyst measures. Red-beryl scores. Citrine warns distance. Reject a decorative hue for a chip count.
  3. Type is weight and size, never a decorative hue. Reject coloring a timestamp amethyst to make it secondary.
  4. Hairline elevates in-flow UI; shadow is for depth viz and floating controls. Reject a shadow on the KPI pill.
  5. Motion explains state. Ceremony may boast. The feed may not. Reject a looping shimmer on the feed.
