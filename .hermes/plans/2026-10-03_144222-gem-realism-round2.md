> **For Hermes:** Use subagent-driven-development skill to implement this plan task-by-task. Each task embeds a quality rating (rubric criteria) and a subagent recipe. Run the loop: score → fix lowest → re-score → repeat.

**Goal:** Fix user's five realism complaints against the procedural gem rig, tracked by the existing rubric instrument (`prototype/gems/rudric.md` is `rubric.md` — 11 criteria, 0–110 per stone).

**Tech Stack:** classic-script THREE r160 rig, `onBeforeCompile` patches (`gem-shader.js`), scratch subagent shots with Playwright/WebKit where noted, metrics via Python pixel probes.

**Quality rating tail:** Track per-task the rubric criteria it targets and the score movement. Score every phase end → target totals.

## Phase 2 — True dispersion (matches real-world gem physics) {#phase-2}

### Task 2: Split the spectrum per-facet, not as a global tint {#phase-2}

**Objective:** Real gems disperse because different IOR per wavelength bends rays differently — fringes appear at facet boundaries and *caustic streaks* at highlight exits, not a hue re-color of the whole stone. Current shipped patch samples two PMREM azimuths and reads uniform.

**Root cause to fix (evidence `card-sapphire-nodisp`):** FIRE_AZ × FIRE_OFF produces roughly the same tint everywhere in the mask. What real fire looks like: spectral separation localized inside *a few pixel rows* near the facet's glint exit.

**Approach:** Rework `GEM_SHADER.dispersion` (src/components/GemReward/gem-shader.js, 90 lines) to (a) sample the PMREM env only where `(reflect)` confirms a glint is present (threshold the specular mask before offsetting), (b) sample R/g/B at three tangent offsets along the mirror angle rather than two, (c) normalize output energy so the mean tint of the stone is *unchanged* (mask the C7 tinted-look penalty) while local hue spread grows.

Subagent (Playwright chromium shot): capture 3 shots — attribution A/B: shipped, tint-off, new. Measure `fire_px_frac`, `fire_dev_p95`, `hue p90−p10`, plus the rubric's on/off probe. Target: fire_px_frac ≥ 0.06, hue p90−p10 ≥ 30°, mean tint shift ≤ 2°.

Files: `gem-shader.js` (modified), `.hermes/tmp/rubric-replay/dispersion/`
Verify: all three metrics move; probe noise band respected; `lint:tokens` green; side-by-side vision read: "fire reads as sparkly spectral streaks, not a red/blue film".
