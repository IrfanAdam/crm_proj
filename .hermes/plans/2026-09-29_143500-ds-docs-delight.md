- **Fixed header offset:** Forgetting `padding-top` on `.wrap` hides first hero under header — covered by `calc(56px + 24px)`.

- **File budget:** Header CSS tends to grow; keep `gallery-chrome.css` ≤100 by not re-adding title/search rules.
- **Motion sub-tabs:** Must stay `btn.after` — reverting to `grp.appendChild` re-breaks placement at end of Foundations.
