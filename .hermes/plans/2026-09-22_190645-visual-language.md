# Visual Language — paper, glass, ceremony

> **For Hermes:** Use subagent-driven-development skill to implement this plan task-by-task.

**Goal:** Turn the CRM catalog into one visual language — paper content, glass chrome, ceremony quarantined — witnessed by ref-01–12 and proved by the live prototype, at the quality bar of a published system.

**Architecture:** Do not add components and do not restyle glass. Name three registers, add role tokens that alias today's colors (pixel-identical), then make the Opps screen compose those roles instead of forking them. Gallery teaches the language. A gate fails the next fork. Prior plans stay frozen.

**Tech Stack:** Existing DTCG JSON → `scripts/build-tokens.mjs` → `design-system/tokens.css`; vanilla pattern CSS; Phosphor; `npm test` (lint + contrast + plan:names + ds-track). No Storybook, no framework binding, no iOS/Android export.

**Tags:** Design System, Layout, Component, Tooling

### What the prototype actually is

Opps (`mount("Opps")` in `opps-home.js`) follows the ref-01 order and is the strongest DS consumption in the repo: `opp-card` anatomy (sapphire stage, red-beryl chance, sapphire value, amethyst is *not* on the card — distance is citrine-700), 44px targets, focus rings, Phosphor.

It also forks the language:

- **Nav superseded the reference, on purpose.** Dock is 3 tabs (Home / Leads / Opps), glass capsule, signal on the selected icon. ref-01 and phase-3 say 5 glyphs and a position-only dot. The prototype wins. Record the supersession. Do not revert.
- **Glass is chrome, and phase-5 forbids it in the wrong place.** Phase-5 says Operate stays flat. The dock, top edge, and status blur are chrome, not content cards. Amend the sentence. Do not retune `OPTICS` or `dock-lens.css` values.
- **Title uses a reserved step.** `.opps__title` is `--font-size-2xl` (24) at `-0.02em`. Phase-2 locks title at 32 / `--font-size-3xl` / `-0.01em`, and says 24 is reserved, not a hierarchy step. This is the one intentional visual change in the plan.
- **In-flow elevation drifted.** `.opps__kpi` carries `--shadow-sm`. Phase-2: hairline carries UI; shadow is for depth and for controls floating on imagery. The map pill (`--shadow-md` on `.opps__nearby`) may keep its shadow. The KPI pill may not.
- **Chips are a second component.** `.opps__chip` duplicates `.chip`. Hot state is already the right idea (2px ink). Compose the DS chip; do not restyle the hot outline down to 1px.
- **Activity diamonds are allowed, unnamed.** Phase-3 already specifies a diamond marker on peaked bars. The prototype's outline/fill SVG is that marker, colored amethyst, which matches ref-01/02. It is not a ceremony-gem leak. Name it. Do not delete it. Ceremony (Spline, hue-rotate, capsule copy) stays out of the feed.
- **Home and Leads are not screens.** `EMPTY_HTML` is a dashed box. A language has one empty state.
- **Two icon systems.** Prototype is Phosphor. Gallery identity/nav still uses `sprite.svg`. New work is Phosphor. Do not delete the sprite in this plan.

### What "global standards" means here

The bar of a published system (Material, HIG, Polaris, Carbon, WCAG 2.2) — applied to these references, not copied from them:

1. Few principles that can reject a screen.
2. Roles, not ramp-picking (`--role-measure`, not "amethyst felt right").
3. Named materials with a quarantine (paper / glass / ceremony).
4. Screens composed from those roles. One icon system.
5. Docs that show a composed screen beside the reference, not only a parts bin.
6. A gate that fails the next fork.
7. Contrast pairs on the roles people actually read.

Out of scope, do not re-propose: DTCG pipeline, the atom library, Storybook, a framework binding, iOS/Android token export, a full Reports or Funnel product screen, a dark product theme, glass retunes, deleting `ref-06-*-DUP`, packing files to beat the 100-line cap.

Standing laws that still win: `AGENTS.md` §1–10 (100 lines, no raw color outside generated tokens, one plan trailer, no push). Device geometry (55pt squircle, safe area, SDF bleed) stays in `src/device/`, `src/os/`, `src/glass/optics.js`. It is not a brand radius.
